# Breakie Burgers

Freezer breakfast burgers: English muffin, pork and beef patty, cheese, homemade pickles and hot sauce.

Category: breakfast · 40 min · tags: breakfast, freezer, grab and go

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 1 burger with a double lean patty (160 g raw) | 477 | 41 g |
| Paris | 1 burger with a single patty (80 g raw) | 341 | 25 g |

## Ingredients (makes 6 of Jaidyn's and 6 of Paris's serves)

- 12 whole English muffins
- 1440 g pork and beef mince (80 g patties)
- 12 whole small cheese slices

## Method

1. **Shape.** Shape the mince into 80 g patties, a little wider than the muffins. Season.
2. **Cook.** Cook in a hot pan or on the barbecue about 3 to 4 minutes a side until cooked through. (8 min)
3. **Build.** Muffin, patty (two for Jaidyn's), cheese slice, pickles and hot sauce.
4. **Freeze.** Cool, wrap each burger and freeze. Microwave from frozen about 90 seconds, then toast if you like.

## Notes

Add an egg for about 70 kcal and 6 g protein.
