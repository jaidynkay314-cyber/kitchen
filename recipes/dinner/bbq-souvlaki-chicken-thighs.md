# BBQ Souvlaki Chicken Thighs

Lemon, garlic and oregano chicken thighs off the barbecue, with sweet potato mash, broccoli and a high-protein tzatziki.

Category: dinner · 45 min · tags: chicken, bbq, high protein

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 250 g chicken, about 300 g sweet potato mash, half the broccoli and tzatziki | 794 | 67 g |
| Paris | 150 g chicken, about 200 g sweet potato mash, half the broccoli and tzatziki | 587 | 46 g |

## Ingredients (makes 1 of Jaidyn's and 1 of Paris's serves)

- 400 g chicken thigh fillet, skinless
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 whole lemon, juiced and zested
- 3 clove garlic
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 500 g sweet potato, peeled and chopped
- 10 g butter
- 2 tbsp milk, full cream
- 300 g broccoli, in florets
- 150 g high-protein Greek yoghurt
- 0.5 whole Lebanese cucumber, grated and squeezed dry

## Method

1. **Marinate the chicken.** Mix the oil, lemon juice and zest, two of the garlic cloves, oregano, paprika and salt. Toss the chicken through and leave at least 20 minutes, or up to a day in the fridge. (20 min)
2. **Boil the sweet potato.** Cover the sweet potato with cold salted water, bring to the boil and simmer until a knife slides straight through. (15 min)
3. **Make the tzatziki.** Stir the cucumber and the last garlic clove through the yoghurt with a squeeze of lemon and a pinch of salt. Keep it in the fridge.
4. **Barbecue the chicken.** Cook on a hot barbecue plate or grill, 5 to 6 minutes a side, until charred and cooked through (75°C in the thickest part). (11 min)
5. **Rest, and cook the broccoli.** Rest the chicken 5 minutes. Steam or microwave the broccoli until bright green and just tender. (5 min)
6. **Mash and serve.** Drain the sweet potato and mash with the butter and milk, then season. Slice the chicken and serve with the mash, broccoli and tzatziki.

## Notes

Leftover cooked thighs keep 3 days in the fridge for a lunch wrap.
