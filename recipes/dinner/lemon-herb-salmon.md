# Lemon Herb Salmon with Baby Potatoes and Beans

Oven-baked salmon with lemon and herbs, crispy baby potatoes and green beans. One tray, mostly hands off.

Category: dinner · 40 min · tags: fish, tray bake, omega 3

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 200 g salmon (a large fillet), 250 g potatoes, 125 g beans | 671 | 48 g |
| Paris | 130 g salmon (a regular fillet), 150 g potatoes, 100 g beans | 454 | 32 g |

## Ingredients (makes 1 of Jaidyn's and 1 of Paris's serves)

- 330 g salmon fillet, skin on
- 400 g baby potatoes, halved
- 225 g green beans, trimmed
- 2 tsp olive oil
- 1 whole lemon, zested and sliced
- 2 clove garlic
- 2 tbsp fresh dill or parsley, chopped

## Method

1. **Potatoes in.** Oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Toss the potatoes with the oil, salt and pepper on a lined tray and roast. (20 min)
2. **Season the salmon.** Mix the lemon zest, crushed garlic and herbs. Pat the salmon dry and press the mix on top.
3. **Salmon and beans in.** Push the potatoes to the sides. Add the salmon skin side down, lay lemon slices around, and scatter the beans. Roast until the salmon just flakes. (12 min)
4. **Serve.** Squeeze some of the roasted lemon over everything.

## Notes

Salmon is higher in fat than the other dinners, but it's mostly omega 3. Pull it when the centre is still slightly translucent; it finishes on the plate.
