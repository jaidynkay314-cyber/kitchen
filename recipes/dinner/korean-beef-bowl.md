# Korean Beef Bowl

Sweet, spicy gochujang beef mince over rice with quick-pickled cucumber and a fried egg.

Category: dinner · 25 min · tags: beef, asian, quick

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 200 g raw mince, a cup of cooked rice, 1 egg, half the cucumber | 715 | 56 g |
| Paris | 130 g raw mince, 2/3 cup cooked rice, 1 egg, half the cucumber | 520 | 39 g |

## Ingredients (makes 1 of Jaidyn's and 1 of Paris's serves)

- 330 g lean beef mince (5% fat)
- 1.5 tbsp gochujang
- 1 tbsp soy sauce (salt-reduced)
- 2 tsp honey
- 2 clove garlic
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 0.65 cup jasmine or short-grain rice
- 1 whole Lebanese cucumber, thinly sliced
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 whole eggs
- 2 whole spring onions, sliced

## Method

1. **Rice and pickles.** Put the rice on. Toss the cucumber with the rice vinegar and a pinch each of salt and sugar. Leave it to pickle while you cook. (15 min)
2. **Mix the sauce.** Stir together the gochujang, soy, honey, garlic and ginger.
3. **Cook the beef.** Hot pan, high heat. Brown the mince well, breaking it up, until any liquid cooks off and it starts to crisp. (7 min)
4. **Sauce the beef.** Add the sauce and toss for a minute until sticky. Finish with the sesame oil. (1 min)
5. **Fry the eggs.** Wipe the pan, a little spray oil, and fry the eggs to your liking. (3 min)
6. **Bowl up.** Rice, beef, drained cucumber, egg on top, spring onion over everything.

## Notes

Gochujang is in the Asian aisle at Woolies. It varies in heat by brand, so start with 1 tbsp.
