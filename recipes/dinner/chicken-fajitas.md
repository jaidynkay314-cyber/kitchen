# Chicken Fajitas

Spiced chicken breast with charred capsicum and onion, in mini tortillas.

Category: dinner · 30 min · tags: chicken, mexican, quick

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 4 mini tortillas, 220 g raw chicken, half the veg | 666 | 62 g |
| Paris | 3 mini tortillas, 140 g raw chicken, half the veg | 506 | 42 g |

## Ingredients (makes 1 of Jaidyn's and 1 of Paris's serves)

- 360 g chicken breast, in strips
- 3 tsp Fajita spices (smoked paprika, cumin, garlic powder, oregano, chilli)
- 2 whole capsicums (mixed colours), sliced
- 1 whole red onion, sliced
- 2 tsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- 7 whole mini tortillas
- 0.25 cup salsa

## Method

1. **Season.** Toss the chicken with the spices, half the oil and a pinch of salt.
2. **Char the veg.** Very hot pan or barbecue plate. Cook the capsicum and onion with the rest of the oil until soft and charred at the edges. Set aside. (6 min)
3. **Cook the chicken.** Same pan, high heat. Cook the chicken strips in one layer, turning once, until browned and cooked through. (6 min)
4. **Serve.** Squeeze the lime over the chicken and veg. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan and load them up with the salsa and sauce.

## Notes

Swap a tortilla each for shredded lettuce to cut about 75 kcal.
