# Chimichurri Steak with Corn and Charred Capsicum Salad

Barbecued rump with a bright, light-on-oil chimichurri, corn on the cob and a charred capsicum and rocket salad.

Category: dinner · 35 min · tags: beef, bbq, high protein

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 250 g raw rump (about 190 g cooked), 1 cob of corn, half the salad | 588 | 62 g |
| Paris | 150 g raw rump (about 115 g cooked), 1 cob of corn, half the salad | 448 | 40 g |

## Ingredients (makes 1 of Jaidyn's and 1 of Paris's serves)

- 400 g rump steak, fat trimmed
- 2 whole corn cob
- 2 whole capsicums, quartered
- 0.5 whole red onion, thinly sliced
- 60 g rocket
- 8 tbsp flat-leaf parsley, a big bunch, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 2 clove garlic
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 0.5 tsp chilli flakes

## Method

1. **Make the chimichurri.** Mix the parsley, oil, vinegar, crushed garlic, oregano, chilli flakes, salt and 2 tbsp water. It's better after 15 minutes. (15 min)
2. **Steak out.** Take the steak out to come up to room temperature. Pat dry and season well.
3. **Grill the veg.** Hot barbecue. Grill the corn, turning, until charred in spots, and the capsicum until blistered and soft. (10 min)
4. **Grill the steak.** Cook the rump on the hottest part, 3 to 4 minutes a side for medium rare depending on thickness. (7 min)
5. **Rest and salad.** Rest the steak 5 minutes. Slice the capsicum and toss with the onion, rocket and a spoon of the chimichurri. (5 min)
6. **Serve.** Slice the steak across the grain and spoon chimichurri over the top. Corn and salad on the side.

## Notes

Rump is lean and full of flavour, but it can be chewy: slice thin and across the grain.
