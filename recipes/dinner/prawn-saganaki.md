# Prawn Saganaki

Prawns baked in a garlicky tomato sauce with feta and oregano, with sourdough to mop it up.

Category: dinner · 30 min · tags: seafood, greek, quick

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 200 g raw prawns, half the sauce, 2 slices sourdough | 614 | 58 g |
| Paris | 130 g raw prawns, half the sauce, 1 slice sourdough | 409 | 38 g |

## Ingredients (makes 1 of Jaidyn's and 1 of Paris's serves)

- 330 g raw prawns, peeled
- 1 tin diced tomatoes, tinned
- 1 whole onion, finely diced
- 3 clove garlic
- 2 tsp olive oil
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 0.5 tsp chilli flakes
- 60 g light feta, crumbled
- 2 tbsp parsley or dill, chopped
- 3 slice sourdough

## Method

1. **Sauce.** Oven to 200°C (180°C fan). In an ovenproof pan, cook the onion in the oil until soft, then the garlic, oregano and chilli for a minute. (7 min)
2. **Simmer.** Add the tomatoes and simmer until thick, about 10 minutes. Season. (10 min)
3. **Prawns and feta.** Nestle the prawns into the sauce and crumble the feta over the top.
4. **Bake.** Bake until the prawns are pink and the feta is soft and golden at the edges. (10 min)
5. **Serve.** Scatter with herbs and serve with toasted sourdough.

## Notes

No ovenproof pan? Simmer the prawns in the sauce with the lid on for 3 to 4 minutes, then add the feta.
