# Garlic Chilli Prawn Linguine

Prawns, garlic, chilli, cherry tomatoes and lemon through linguine. Light on the oil, big on flavour.

Category: dinner · 25 min · tags: seafood, pasta, quick

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 200 g raw prawns, 100 g dry pasta (about 2 cups cooked) | 689 | 60 g |
| Paris | 130 g raw prawns, 65 g dry pasta (about 1.3 cups cooked) | 490 | 40 g |

## Ingredients (makes 1 of Jaidyn's and 1 of Paris's serves)

- 330 g raw prawns, peeled
- 165 g linguine
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 4 clove garlic, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp chilli flakes (to taste)
- 250 g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 whole lemon, zested and juiced
- 60 g baby spinach
- 20 g parmesan, grated
- 2 tbsp parsley, chopped

## Method

1. **Pasta on.** Cook the linguine in well-salted water. Save a mug of pasta water before draining. (10 min)
2. **Garlic and chilli.** Meanwhile, warm the oil in a big pan on medium. Add the garlic and chilli and cook gently until the garlic is just golden. (2 min)
3. **Tomatoes.** Turn up the heat, add the tomatoes and cook until they start to burst. (3 min)
4. **Prawns.** Add the prawns and cook until pink and curled, about 2 minutes. Don't overcook them. (2 min)
5. **Toss it all.** Add the drained pasta, spinach, lemon zest and juice and a good splash of pasta water. Toss until glossy and the spinach wilts. Top with parmesan and parsley.

## Notes

The pasta water does the job extra oil would do. Use it.
