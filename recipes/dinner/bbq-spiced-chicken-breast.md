# BBQ Spiced Chicken Breast with Sweet Potato Mash and Broccolini

Lean weeknight barbecue: dry-rubbed chicken breast, sweet potato mash and broccolini. Good with a yoghurt sauce.

Category: dinner · 35 min · tags: chicken, bbq, high protein, lean

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | On the plate: about 190 g cooked chicken (250 g raw), 300 g mash (a heaped cup and a half), 100 g broccolini | 563 | 66 g |
| Paris | On the plate: about 120 g cooked chicken (160 g raw), 175 g mash (a cup), 75 g broccolini | 352 | 43 g |

## Ingredients (makes 1 of Jaidyn's and 1 of Paris's serves)

- 410 g chicken breast
- 2 tsp Spices (paprika, garlic and onion powder, whatever you like)
- 475 g sweet potato, peeled and chopped
- 2 tbsp trim milk, for the mash
- 1 bunch broccolini (or 175 g broccoli)

## Method

1. **Rub the chicken.** If the breasts are thick, butterfly them or pound to an even thickness so they cook evenly without drying out. Coat both sides with the spices and a pinch of salt.
2. **Boil the sweet potato.** Cover with cold salted water, bring to the boil and simmer until a knife slides straight through. (15 min)
3. **Barbecue the chicken.** Hot barbecue, no oil needed on a clean, well-heated grill. About 5 to 6 minutes a side until it hits 74°C in the thickest part. Pull it the moment it gets there: that's what keeps breast juicy. (11 min)
4. **Rest, and cook the broccolini.** Rest the chicken 5 minutes under foil. Steam or microwave the broccolini until bright green and just tender, or throw it on the barbecue for the last 3 minutes. (5 min)
5. **Mash and serve.** Drain the sweet potato, mash with the milk and season. Slice the chicken and plate up with the mash, broccolini and your sauce.

## Notes

Raw chicken breast loses about a quarter of its weight cooking, so 250 g raw is about 190 g on the plate. Weigh raw if you can; the cooked weights are a guide. A knob of butter in the mash adds about 70 kcal.
