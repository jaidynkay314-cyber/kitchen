# Tandoori Chicken with Cucumber Salad and Naan

Yoghurt-marinated tandoori chicken thighs off the barbecue, with a crunchy cucumber and onion salad and a mini naan.

Category: dinner · 40 min · tags: chicken, bbq, indian

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 220 g raw chicken, 1 mini naan, half the salad | 492 | 55 g |
| Paris | 140 g raw chicken, 1 mini naan, half the salad | 395 | 39 g |

## Ingredients (makes 1 of Jaidyn's and 1 of Paris's serves)

- 360 g chicken thigh fillet, skinless
- 100 g Greek yoghurt, for the marinade
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp chilli powder and turmeric, to taste
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 clove garlic
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 whole Lebanese cucumber, diced
- 0.5 whole red onion, finely sliced
- 2 tbsp fresh mint or coriander
- 2 whole mini naan

## Method

1. **Marinate.** Mix the yoghurt, spices, lemon juice, garlic, ginger and salt. Slash the chicken a few times and coat well. At least 30 minutes, ideally overnight. (30 min)
2. **Salad.** Toss the cucumber, onion and mint with a squeeze of lemon and a pinch of salt.
3. **Grill.** Hot barbecue. Cook the chicken until charred at the edges and cooked through, about 5 to 6 minutes a side. (11 min)
4. **Naan and serve.** Warm the naan on the barbecue for the last minute. Serve with the salad and tzatziki. (1 min)

## Notes

The yoghurt marinade is what keeps the thighs so tender. Don't skip the slashes.
