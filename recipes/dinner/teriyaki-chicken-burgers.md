# Teriyaki Chicken Burgers

Grilled teriyaki chicken thigh fillets with charred pineapple and crunchy Asian slaw in a soft bun.

Category: dinner · 30 min · tags: chicken, bbq, fakeaway

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 1 burger with 2 thigh fillets (about 180 g raw) | 493 | 44 g |
| Paris | 1 burger with 1 large thigh fillet (about 120 g raw) | 415 | 32 g |

## Ingredients (makes 1 of Jaidyn's and 1 of Paris's serves)

- 300 g chicken thigh fillet, skinless
- 3 tbsp teriyaki sauce
- 0.5 tin pineapple rings or pieces in juice
- 2 whole burger buns
- 120 g coleslaw mix
- 2 tsp lime juice, for the slaw
- 0.5 tsp sesame oil

## Method

1. **Marinate.** Flatten the thighs a little and toss in 2 tbsp of the teriyaki. 15 minutes is enough. (15 min)
2. **Slaw.** Toss the slaw mix with the lime juice, sesame oil and a pinch of salt.
3. **Grill.** Medium-high barbecue. Cook the chicken about 5 minutes a side, brushing with the last of the teriyaki in the final minute. Grill the pineapple until charred. (10 min)
4. **Build.** Toast the buns. Chicken, pineapple, slaw and sauce.
