# Baked Salmon with Couscous and Broccolini

Plain salmon fillets baked until browned, on fluffy couscous with broccolini and a Greek yoghurt sauce. Easy weeknight omega 3.

Category: dinner · 25 min · tags: fish, omega 3, quick

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 200 g salmon (a large fillet), about 1 cup cooked couscous, 100 g broccolini | 687 | 52 g |
| Paris | 130 g salmon (a regular fillet), about 2/3 cup cooked couscous, 90 g broccolini | 454 | 35 g |

## Ingredients (makes 1 of Jaidyn's and 1 of Paris's serves)

- 330 g salmon fillets
- 115 g couscous (dry)
- 190 g broccolini, trimmed

## Method

1. **Salmon in.** Oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Pat the salmon dry and put it skin side down on a lined tray, nothing on it. Bake until browned on top and it just flakes in the middle. (15 min)
2. **Couscous.** While it bakes, boil the kettle. Put the couscous in a bowl with a pinch of salt, pour over the same volume of boiling water (about 3/4 cup for both of you), cover with a plate and leave 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork. (5 min)
3. **Broccolini.** Steam or microwave the broccolini with a splash of water for 3 to 4 minutes, until bright green and just tender. (4 min)
4. **Serve.** Couscous on the plate, broccolini alongside, salmon on top and the sauce over or beside it.

## Notes

Salmon is higher in fat than the other dinners, but it's mostly omega 3. Thicker fillets may need 2 to 3 more minutes. Wholemeal couscous cooks the same way and roughly doubles the fibre.
