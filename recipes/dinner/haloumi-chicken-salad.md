# Haloumi and Chicken Salad with Roast Pumpkin and Quinoa

A big warm salad: grilled chicken and light haloumi with roast pumpkin, quinoa, rocket and cherry tomatoes.

Category: dinner · 40 min · tags: chicken, salad, high protein

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 180 g raw chicken, 50 g haloumi, 3/4 cup cooked quinoa, half the veg | 685 | 64 g |
| Paris | 120 g raw chicken, 30 g haloumi, 1/2 cup cooked quinoa, half the veg | 519 | 43 g |

## Ingredients (makes 1 of Jaidyn's and 1 of Paris's serves)

- 300 g chicken breast
- 80 g light haloumi, sliced
- 0.42 cup quinoa
- 400 g butternut pumpkin, in 2 cm cubes
- 1 whole red onion, in wedges
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp Spices (cumin, smoked paprika)
- 80 g rocket
- 150 g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 tbsp lemon juice

## Method

1. **Roast the pumpkin.** Oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Toss the pumpkin and onion with half the oil, the spices and salt, and roast until golden. (25 min)
2. **Quinoa.** Rinse the quinoa and simmer in twice its volume of water until the water's absorbed. Fluff and cool a little. (15 min)
3. **Chicken.** Season the chicken and cook on a hot pan or barbecue, about 4 to 5 minutes a side. Rest and slice. (9 min)
4. **Haloumi.** Same pan, dry, cook the haloumi until golden on both sides. It goes squeaky and rubbery if it cools, so do it last. (3 min)
5. **Toss and serve.** Toss the rocket, tomatoes, quinoa and pumpkin with the lemon juice and the rest of the oil. Top with the chicken and haloumi.

## Notes

Leftover roast pumpkin and quinoa make this a quick work lunch.
