# Chicken Caesar Wraps

Grilled chicken, crispy short-cut bacon, cos and parmesan with a creamy Greek yoghurt caesar dressing, rolled in wholemeal wraps.

Category: dinner · 25 min · tags: chicken, quick, high protein

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 2 wraps: 200 g raw chicken, 40 g bacon | 725 | 76 g |
| Paris | 1 big wrap: 130 g raw chicken, 20 g bacon | 435 | 49 g |

## Ingredients (makes 1 of Jaidyn's and 1 of Paris's serves)

- 330 g chicken breast
- 60 g short-cut bacon
- 3 whole wholemeal wraps
- 1 whole cos lettuce, shredded
- 20 g parmesan, grated
- 100 g Greek yoghurt, for the dressing
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 clove small garlic clove, grated
- 1 tsp mustard (Dijon or wholegrain)
- 1 tsp Spices for the chicken (garlic powder, paprika, pepper)

## Method

1. **Dressing.** Mix the yoghurt, lemon juice, garlic, mustard, half the parmesan, black pepper and a splash of water to make it pourable.
2. **Cook the chicken.** Flatten the breasts to an even thickness, season with the spices and cook on a hot pan or barbecue, about 4 to 5 minutes a side. (9 min)
3. **Crisp the bacon.** Same pan, cook the bacon until crisp, then chop it. (4 min)
4. **Assemble.** Rest and slice the chicken. Toss the cos in most of the dressing. Fill the wraps with lettuce, chicken, bacon and the rest of the parmesan, drizzle the last of the dressing and roll tight.

## Notes

Leftover chicken makes these a 5-minute lunch the next day.
