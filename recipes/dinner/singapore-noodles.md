# Singapore Noodles

Curried rice vermicelli stir-fried with prawns, chicken, egg, capsicum and spring onion.

Category: dinner · 30 min · tags: seafood, chicken, asian, fakeaway

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 80 g dry noodles, 100 g prawns, 100 g chicken, 1 egg | 674 | 57 g |
| Paris | 55 g dry noodles, 65 g prawns, 65 g chicken, 1 egg | 518 | 41 g |

## Ingredients (makes 1 of Jaidyn's and 1 of Paris's serves)

- 135 g rice vermicelli
- 165 g raw prawns, peeled
- 165 g chicken breast, thinly sliced
- 2 whole eggs
- 1 whole red capsicum, thinly sliced
- 3 whole spring onions, in 4 cm lengths
- 2 clove garlic
- 2 tsp curry powder
- 2 tbsp soy sauce (salt-reduced)
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 2 tsp oil

## Method

1. **Soak the noodles.** Cover the vermicelli with boiling water for 3 to 4 minutes, drain, rinse cold and snip into shorter lengths with scissors. (4 min)
2. **Egg.** Hot wok, a drop of the oil. Scramble the eggs into a thin omelette, roll it up and slice. Set aside. (2 min)
3. **Chicken and prawns.** Add the rest of the oil, cook the chicken until nearly done, then the prawns until pink. Set aside. (5 min)
4. **Veg and spice.** Stir-fry the capsicum, garlic and spring onion 1 minute, then add the curry powder for 30 seconds. (2 min)
5. **Toss it together.** Add the noodles, soy, oyster sauce, sesame oil and a splash of water and toss until evenly yellow. Return the chicken, prawns and egg and toss through. (2 min)

## Notes

Tongs are your friend here: lift and toss rather than stir, or the noodles clump.
