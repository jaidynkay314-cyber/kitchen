# Porterhouse with Sweet Potato Mash and Broccolini

A quick Sunday steak night: porterhouse, a fist of sweet potato mash and broccolini.

Category: dinner · 25 min · tags: beef, quick, high protein

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 2 steaks (2 × 200 g), a fist of mash, broccolini | 995 | 90 g |
| Paris | 1 steak (200 g), a smaller fist of mash, broccolini | 589 | 47 g |

## Ingredients (makes 1 of Jaidyn's and 1 of Paris's serves)

- 600 g porterhouse steaks (200 g each)
- 270 g sweet potato
- 10 g butter
- 1 bunch broccolini
- 2 tsp olive oil
- 0.5 tsp salt

## Method

1. **Bring the steak to room temp.** Take the steaks out 20 minutes before cooking. Pat dry and season well. (20 min)
2. **Boil the sweet potato.** Peel, chop and simmer the sweet potato until soft. (15 min)
3. **Cook the steak.** Rub with oil and cook on a very hot pan or barbecue, about 3 to 4 minutes a side for medium rare. (7 min)
4. **Rest and finish.** Rest the steak 5 minutes. Steam the broccolini and mash the sweet potato with the butter. (5 min)

## Notes

Fat trimmed off the steak saves about 80 kcal per 200 g.
