# Smoky BBQ Pulled Chicken Sliders

Slow cooker pulled chicken thighs in smoky barbecue sauce, in slider buns with a yoghurt slaw. Makes two nights' worth.

Category: dinner · 20 min · tags: chicken, slow cooker, fakeaway

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 2 sliders loaded with 250 g raw chicken (about 190 g pulled), plus slaw | 603 | 59 g |
| Paris | 1 slider plus a bowl of chicken and slaw: 150 g raw chicken | 360 | 36 g |

## Ingredients (makes 2 of Jaidyn's and 2 of Paris's serves)

- 800 g chicken thigh fillet, skinless
- 4 tbsp BBQ sauce
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 clove garlic
- 1 whole brown onion, sliced
- 1 tbsp vinegar (apple cider or red wine)
- 6 whole slider buns
- 300 g coleslaw mix
- 60 g Greek yoghurt, for the slaw

## Method

1. **Into the slow cooker.** Onion on the bottom, chicken on top. Mix the BBQ sauce, paprika, garlic, vinegar and a pinch of salt and pour over. No extra liquid.
2. **Cook.** Low for 6 hours or high for 3 to 4, until it falls apart.
3. **Pull.** Shred the chicken with two forks right in the sauce and stir it through. Taste and add a splash more BBQ sauce if you like.
4. **Slaw.** Toss the slaw mix with the yoghurt, a squeeze of lemon, salt and pepper.
5. **Serve.** Warm the buns and load them up with chicken and slaw.

## Notes

Half the batch is the next night (or lunches). Lettuce cups instead of buns save about 110 kcal each.
