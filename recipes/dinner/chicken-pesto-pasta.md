# Chicken Pesto Pasta

Chicken, light basil pesto, cherry tomatoes and spinach through pasta. A 20-minute weeknight.

Category: dinner · 20 min · tags: chicken, pasta, quick

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 180 g raw chicken, 90 g dry pasta (about 2 cups cooked) | 650 | 59 g |
| Paris | 120 g raw chicken, 60 g dry pasta (about 1.3 cups cooked) | 443 | 40 g |

## Ingredients (makes 1 of Jaidyn's and 1 of Paris's serves)

- 300 g chicken breast, sliced
- 150 g pasta (penne or spirals)
- 3 tbsp light basil pesto
- 200 g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 60 g baby spinach
- 1 clove garlic
- 15 g parmesan, grated

## Method

1. **Pasta on.** Cook the pasta in well-salted water. Save a mug of pasta water. (10 min)
2. **Chicken.** Season and cook the chicken in a hot non-stick pan until golden and cooked through. Set aside. (6 min)
3. **Tomatoes and garlic.** Same pan, cook the tomatoes and garlic until the tomatoes just soften. (2 min)
4. **Toss.** Off the heat, add the drained pasta, chicken, pesto, spinach and a splash of pasta water. Toss until glossy and the spinach wilts. Top with parmesan.

## Notes

Pesto on a hot pan goes dark and bitter. Off the heat keeps it bright.
