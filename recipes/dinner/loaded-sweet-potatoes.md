# Loaded Sweet Potatoes with Chilli Beef

Baked sweet potatoes split and loaded with a quick chilli beef and bean mince, cheese and spring onion.

Category: dinner · 50 min · tags: beef, mexican, high protein

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 1 large sweet potato (350 g), 180 g raw mince worth of chilli, 18 g cheese | 729 | 56 g |
| Paris | 1 medium sweet potato (250 g), 120 g raw mince worth of chilli, 12 g cheese | 515 | 38 g |

## Ingredients (makes 1 of Jaidyn's and 1 of Paris's serves)

- 600 g sweet potatoes, 1 each, scrubbed
- 300 g lean beef mince (5% fat)
- 0.5 whole onion, diced
- 2 clove garlic
- 2 tsp Chilli spices (cumin, smoked paprika, chipotle or chilli)
- 0.5 tin kidney beans, drained (half a tin)
- 0.5 tin diced tomatoes (half a tin)
- 30 g reduced-fat cheese, grated
- 2 whole spring onions, sliced

## Method

1. **Bake the sweet potatoes.** Oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Prick the sweet potatoes all over and bake on a tray until completely soft. (Or microwave 8 to 10 minutes, then 10 minutes in the oven to crisp the skin.) (45 min)
2. **Brown the mince.** Hot pan, brown the mince with the onion until any liquid cooks off. (7 min)
3. **Chilli.** Add the garlic and spices for a minute, then the beans and tomatoes. Simmer until thick. (10 min)
4. **Load and serve.** Split the sweet potatoes, fluff the insides with a fork, pile in the chilli, then cheese and spring onion.

## Notes

The chilli doubles easily and freezes, for an even faster version next time.
