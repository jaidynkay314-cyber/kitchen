# Greek Chicken Tray Bake

Lemon, garlic and oregano chicken thighs roasted with potatoes, capsicum and red onion, finished with feta.

Category: dinner · 50 min · tags: chicken, tray bake, greek

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 220 g raw chicken, 250 g potatoes, half the veg, 30 g feta | 640 | 56 g |
| Paris | 140 g raw chicken, 150 g potatoes, half the veg, 20 g feta | 454 | 36 g |

## Ingredients (makes 1 of Jaidyn's and 1 of Paris's serves)

- 360 g chicken thigh fillet, skinless
- 400 g baby potatoes, halved
- 1 whole red capsicum, in chunks
- 1 whole red onion, in wedges
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 whole lemon, juiced
- 3 clove garlic
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 50 g light feta, crumbled

## Method

1. **Mix the marinade.** Oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Whisk the oil, lemon juice, crushed garlic, oregano, salt and pepper.
2. **Potatoes first.** Toss the potatoes in a third of the marinade on a big lined tray and roast. (15 min)
3. **Everything else.** Toss the chicken, capsicum and onion in the rest of the marinade and add to the tray in a single layer. Roast until the chicken is golden and cooked through. (25 min)
4. **Feta and serve.** Crumble the feta over and give it 2 more minutes. Serve with tzatziki. (2 min)

## Notes

Use two trays if it's crowded; crowded trays steam instead of roast.
