# Chicken Gyros

Garlic, lemon and oregano chicken thighs, sliced and wrapped in warm pita with tomato, onion, lettuce and tzatziki.

Category: dinner · 35 min · tags: chicken, greek, fakeaway

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 2 pitas with 200 g raw chicken | 710 | 57 g |
| Paris | 1 pita with 130 g raw chicken (the extra chicken on the side) | 433 | 37 g |

## Ingredients (makes 1 of Jaidyn's and 1 of Paris's serves)

- 330 g chicken thigh fillet, skinless
- 50 g Greek yoghurt, for the marinade
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 3 clove garlic
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 3 whole pita bread
- 2 whole tomatoes, sliced
- 0.5 whole red onion, thinly sliced
- 0.5 whole cos lettuce, shredded

## Method

1. **Marinate.** Mix the yoghurt, lemon juice, crushed garlic, oregano, paprika and salt. Coat the chicken and leave at least 20 minutes, or overnight. (20 min)
2. **Cook.** Hot barbecue or grill pan. Cook the chicken until charred and cooked through, about 5 minutes a side. (10 min)
3. **Slice.** Rest 5 minutes, then slice thinly. (5 min)
4. **Wrap.** Warm the pitas. Load with chicken, tomato, onion, lettuce and tzatziki, and roll up in baking paper to hold it together.
