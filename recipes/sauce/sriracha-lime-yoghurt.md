# Sriracha Lime Yoghurt

Creamy chilli kick. Good on fried rice, chicken bowls and the frittata muffins.

Category: sauce · 5 min · tags: sauce, asian

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 3 tablespoons | 10.0 | 1.4 g |
| Paris | 2 tablespoons | 10.0 | 1.4 g |

Per tablespoon: 10 kcal, 1.4 g protein (macros above are per tablespoon).

## Ingredients (makes about 12 tbsp)

- 170 g Greek yoghurt
- 1 tbsp sriracha (more to taste)
- 2 tsp lime juice

## Method

1. **Mix.** Stir together. Keeps 4 days.

## Notes

Made with high-protein Greek yoghurt (about 60 kcal and 10 g protein per 100 g). Full-fat Greek yoghurt roughly doubles the calories and halves the protein.
