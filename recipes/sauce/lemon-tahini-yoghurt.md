# Lemon Tahini Yoghurt

Nutty and creamy. Great with chicken, lamb or roast veg. A bit richer than the others.

Category: sauce · 5 min · tags: sauce, chicken, lamb

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 3 tablespoons | 15.5 | 1.5 g |
| Paris | 2 tablespoons | 15.5 | 1.5 g |

Per tablespoon: 16 kcal, 1.5 g protein (macros above are per tablespoon).

## Ingredients (makes about 13 tbsp)

- 170 g Greek yoghurt
- 1 tbsp tahini
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 clove small garlic clove, grated

## Method

1. **Mix.** Whisk the tahini with the lemon juice first (it thickens, then loosens), then stir through the yoghurt and garlic. Thin with a splash of water. Keeps 4 days.

## Notes

Made with high-protein Greek yoghurt (about 60 kcal and 10 g protein per 100 g). Full-fat Greek yoghurt roughly doubles the calories and halves the protein.
