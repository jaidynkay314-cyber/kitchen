# Garlic Herb Yoghurt

Garlicky yoghurt with lemon and fresh herbs. Good with steak, chicken and roast veg.

Category: sauce · 5 min · tags: sauce, beef, chicken

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 3 tablespoons | 10.3 | 1.5 g |
| Paris | 2 tablespoons | 10.3 | 1.5 g |

Per tablespoon: 10 kcal, 1.5 g protein (macros above are per tablespoon).

## Ingredients (makes about 12 tbsp)

- 170 g Greek yoghurt
- 2 clove garlic, finely grated
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp parsley or chives, chopped

## Method

1. **Mix.** Stir everything together with salt and pepper. Let it sit 10 minutes so the garlic mellows. Keeps 3 days.

## Notes

Made with high-protein Greek yoghurt (about 60 kcal and 10 g protein per 100 g). Full-fat Greek yoghurt roughly doubles the calories and halves the protein.
