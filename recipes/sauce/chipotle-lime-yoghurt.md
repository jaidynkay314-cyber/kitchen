# Chipotle Lime Yoghurt

Smoky, a little hot. Made for the burritos, chilli and anything Mexican.

Category: sauce · 5 min · tags: sauce, mexican

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 3 tablespoons | 9.5 | 1.5 g |
| Paris | 2 tablespoons | 9.5 | 1.5 g |

Per tablespoon: 10 kcal, 1.5 g protein (macros above are per tablespoon).

## Ingredients (makes about 12 tbsp)

- 170 g Greek yoghurt
- 1 tsp chipotle powder (half for mild)
- 2 tsp lime juice
- 0.25 tsp pinch of ground cumin

## Method

1. **Mix.** Stir everything together with a pinch of salt. It gets hotter as it sits. Keeps 4 days.

## Notes

Made with high-protein Greek yoghurt (about 60 kcal and 10 g protein per 100 g). Full-fat Greek yoghurt roughly doubles the calories and halves the protein.
