# Mustard Lime Yoghurt

Jaidyn's sauce: Greek yoghurt, wholegrain mustard and lime. Fresh and sharp, great on barbecued chicken.

Category: sauce · 5 min · tags: sauce, chicken

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 3 tablespoons | 10.8 | 1.5 g |
| Paris | 2 tablespoons | 10.8 | 1.5 g |

Per tablespoon: 11 kcal, 1.5 g protein (macros above are per tablespoon).

## Ingredients (makes about 12 tbsp)

- 170 g Greek yoghurt
- 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard
- 3 tsp lime juice
- 1 tsp spices to taste

## Method

1. **Mix.** Stir everything together and season with salt and pepper. Taste and add more lime or mustard as you like. Keeps 4 days in the fridge.

## Notes

Made with high-protein Greek yoghurt (about 60 kcal and 10 g protein per 100 g). Full-fat Greek yoghurt roughly doubles the calories and halves the protein.
