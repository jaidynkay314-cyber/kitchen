# Tzatziki

Greek yoghurt, cucumber, garlic, lemon and dill or mint.

Category: sauce · 5 min · tags: sauce, greek, chicken, lamb

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 3 tablespoons | 8.7 | 1.3 g |

Per tablespoon: 9 kcal, 1.3 g protein (macros above are per tablespoon).

## Ingredients (makes about 14 tbsp)

- 170 g Greek yoghurt
- 0.5 whole Lebanese cucumber, grated and squeezed dry
- 1 clove garlic, finely grated
- 2 tsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp fresh dill or mint, chopped

## Method

1. **Squeeze the cucumber.** Grate the cucumber and squeeze it hard in your hands or a clean tea towel. This stops the tzatziki going watery.
2. **Mix.** Stir everything into the yoghurt with a pinch of salt. Best after 10 minutes in the fridge. Keeps 3 days.

## Notes

Made with high-protein Greek yoghurt (about 60 kcal and 10 g protein per 100 g). Full-fat Greek yoghurt roughly doubles the calories and halves the protein.
