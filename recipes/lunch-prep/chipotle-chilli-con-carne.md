# Chipotle Chilli Con Carne

Smoky chilli with rice. Post-gym or rest days.

Category: lunch prep · 50 min · tags: beef, pork, freezer, rice

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | a sixth of the chilli (weigh the pot, divide by 6) and 3/4 cup cooked rice | 601 | 45 g |
| Paris | 2/3 of Jaidyn's chilli scoop and 1/2 cup cooked rice | 401 | 30 g |

## Ingredients (makes 4 of Jaidyn's and 3 of Paris's serves)

- 1000 g lean pork and beef mince (50/50)
- 2 tin kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 2 tin diced tomatoes, tinned
- 1 whole brown onion, diced
- 1 whole red capsicum, diced
- 4 clove garlic
- 2 tsp chipotle powder
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1.5 cup brown rice, uncooked

## Method

1. **Brown the mince.** Brown the mince in a large pot over medium-high heat, breaking it up. No extra oil needed. (10 min)
2. **Soften the veg.** Add the onion, capsicum and garlic and cook 4 to 5 minutes. (5 min)
3. **Bloom the spices.** Stir in all the spices and cook 1 minute until fragrant. (1 min)
4. **Simmer.** Add the tomatoes and beans and simmer uncovered until thick, stirring now and then. (25 min)
5. **Cook the rice.** Cook the rice separately. Season the chilli and add your hot sauce. Brown rice takes about 30 minutes. (30 min)
6. **Portion.** Weigh the chilli and divide by 6 for one regular serve. Pack 4 regular (3/4 cup rice) and 3 small (2/3 chilli, 1/2 cup rice).

## Notes

The batch makes exactly 4 regular and 3 small serves.
