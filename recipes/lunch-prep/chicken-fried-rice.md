# Chicken Fried Rice

Freezer-friendly fried rice. Best as a pre-gym lunch: lower in fat.

Category: lunch prep · 40 min · tags: chicken, freezer, rice

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 1 regular container (a sixth of the batch) | 568 | 53 g |
| Paris | 2/3 of a regular container | 379 | 36 g |

## Ingredients (makes 4 of Jaidyn's and 3 of Paris's serves)

- 1000 g chicken breast, diced small
- 2 cup brown rice (cook the day before)
- 6 whole eggs
- 500 g frozen mixed veg
- 4 whole spring onions, sliced
- 4 clove garlic
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 80 ml soy sauce (salt-reduced if you like)
- 1 tbsp oil

## Method

1. **Cook the chicken.** Heat half the oil in a wok on high and cook the chicken in two batches until browned and cooked through. Set aside. (10 min)
2. **Scramble the eggs.** Scramble the eggs in the same pan, break them up and set aside with the chicken. (2 min)
3. **Aromatics and veg.** Add the rest of the oil, the garlic and ginger. Stir 30 seconds, add the frozen veg and cook 3 to 4 minutes. (4 min)
4. **Fry the rice.** Add the cold rice and toss on high heat for 4 to 5 minutes, breaking up clumps. (5 min)
5. **Combine.** Return the chicken and egg, add the soy sauce and spring onion and toss through.
6. **Portion.** Spread on a tray to cool fast, then portion: 4 regular and 3 small containers.

## Notes

Keeps 2 to 3 months frozen. Reheat until steaming hot all the way through.
