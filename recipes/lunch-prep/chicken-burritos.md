# Chicken Burritos

Grab-and-go freezer burritos for hungrier days.

Category: lunch prep · 45 min · tags: chicken, freezer, grab and go

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 1 large burrito | 661 | 55 g |
| Paris | 1 burrito on a regular tortilla with 2/3 of the filling | 441 | 37 g |

## Ingredients (makes 4 of Jaidyn's and 3 of Paris's serves)

- 1000 g chicken breast
- 1.5 cup brown rice, uncooked
- 1 tin black beans, drained and rinsed
- 120 g grated cheese
- 4 whole large tortillas
- 3 whole regular tortillas
- 0.5 cup salsa
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp chipotle powder
- 1 whole lime (optional)

## Method

1. **Cook the chicken.** Rub the chicken with the spices and bake at 200°C until cooked through. Rest 5 minutes, then shred with two forks. (22 min)
2. **Cook the rice.** Cook the rice and stir through the lime juice and a pinch of salt. Brown rice takes about 30 minutes. (30 min)
3. **Mix the filling.** Toss the chicken with the beans, salsa and hot sauce. Let everything cool before rolling.
4. **Roll.** Large tortillas: rice, chicken mix, cheese. Regular tortillas: about 2/3 as much of each. Fold the sides in and roll tightly.
5. **Wrap and freeze.** Wrap each one in cling wrap or foil, then bag and freeze.

## Notes

Microwave from frozen 3 to 4 minutes (foil off), turning halfway.
