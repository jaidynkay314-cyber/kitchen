# Lean Beef and Bean Chilli

No-rice chilli: high protein, lower calorie.

Category: friday · 45 min · tags: beef, freezer, low calorie

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 1 container (a sixth of the batch) | 392 | 43 g |
| Paris | 2/3 of a container | 261 | 29 g |

## Ingredients (makes 6 of Jaidyn's serves)

- 1000 g lean beef mince (5% fat)
- 2 tin kidney beans, tinned (drained)
- 2 tin diced tomatoes, tinned
- 1 whole brown onion
- 1 whole red capsicum
- 3 clove garlic
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp chipotle powder
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tbsp olive oil

## Method

1. **Soften.** Heat the oil and soften the onion and capsicum, then add the garlic and spices for 1 minute. (6 min)
2. **Brown.** Add the mince and brown, breaking it up. (8 min)
3. **Simmer.** Add the tomatoes, beans and half a cup of water. Simmer uncovered until thick. (28 min)
4. **Portion.** Season, portion into 6 containers, cool and freeze.

## Notes

Top with a spoon of high-protein yoghurt instead of sour cream.
