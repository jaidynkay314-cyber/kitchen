# Egg and Veg Frittata Muffins

24 muffins, frozen in fours. Microwave 60 to 90 seconds from frozen.

Category: friday · 35 min · tags: eggs, freezer, low calorie

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 4 muffins | 287 | 34 g |
| Paris | 3 muffins | 215 | 25 g |

## Ingredients (makes 6 of Jaidyn's serves)

- 12 whole eggs
- 500 ml liquid egg whites
- 200 g lean ham or cooked chicken, diced
- 150 g baby spinach
- 1 whole red capsicum
- 4 whole spring onions
- 100 g reduced-fat cheese, grated

## Method

1. **Prep.** Heat the oven to 180°C and spray two 12-hole muffin tins well.
2. **Fill.** Divide the ham, spinach, capsicum and spring onion between the holes.
3. **Pour.** Whisk the eggs, whites, salt and pepper, pour over the fillings about three-quarters full and top with cheese.
4. **Bake.** Bake until set, then cool on a rack. (20 min)
5. **Freeze.** Freeze on a tray, then bag in fours.
