# Beef Meatballs in Tomato and Veg

Oven-baked lean meatballs in a veggie passata. Freezes well.

Category: friday · 40 min · tags: beef, freezer, low calorie

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 5 meatballs with sauce | 365 | 43 g |
| Paris | 3 meatballs with sauce | 219 | 26 g |

## Ingredients (makes 6 of Jaidyn's serves)

- 1000 g lean beef mince (5% fat)
- 1 whole eggs
- 40 g rolled oats
- 0.66 whole small onion, grated
- 2 clove garlic
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 700 g passata
- 2 whole zucchini, diced
- 1 whole red capsicum, diced
- 50 g parmesan, grated

## Method

1. **Roll.** Heat the oven to 200°C. Mix the mince, egg, oats, onion, garlic, oregano, salt and pepper and roll about 30 balls.
2. **Bake.** Bake on lined trays until browned. (17 min)
3. **Sauce.** Meanwhile simmer the passata with the zucchini and capsicum. (10 min)
4. **Combine and portion.** Add the meatballs to the sauce and simmer 5 minutes. Pack 5 meatballs plus sauce per container, top with parmesan, cool and freeze. (5 min)
