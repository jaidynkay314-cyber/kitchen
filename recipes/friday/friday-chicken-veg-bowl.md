# Lean Chicken and Veg Bowl

Light, high-protein freezer lunch for Fridays before beers.

Category: friday · 35 min · tags: chicken, freezer, low calorie

| | Portion | kcal | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaidyn | 1 container (a sixth of the batch) | 388 | 49 g |
| Paris | 2/3 of a container | 259 | 33 g |

## Ingredients (makes 6 of Jaidyn's serves)

- 1100 g chicken breast, diced
- 900 g frozen mixed veg
- 125 g brown rice (about 2/3 cup)
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
- 2 clove garlic
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated

## Method

1. **Cook the rice.** Cook the rice and spread it on a tray to cool. Brown rice takes about 30 minutes. (30 min)
2. **Brown the chicken.** Spray a large pan and brown the chicken in two batches over high heat. (14 min)
3. **Veg.** Add the garlic, ginger and frozen veg and cook until just tender. (5 min)
4. **Sauce.** Return the chicken, add the soy and sweet chilli and toss 1 minute. (1 min)
5. **Portion.** Divide the rice and chicken between 6 containers. Cool, lid and freeze.
